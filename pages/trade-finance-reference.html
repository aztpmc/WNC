    About this reference

    This tool provides original, plain-language summaries of Incoterms® 2020, ICC Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits (UCP 600), and ICC Uniform Rules for Demand Guarantees (URDG 758), plus a topic-level index of ICC International Standard Banking Practice (ISBP 821) — it does not reproduce ICC's copyrighted rule text. It is offline, works entirely in your browser, and sends no data anywhere.

    These summaries are a study and orientation aid, not legal advice and not a substitute for the official ICC publications. Always verify the exact wording of the applicable rule against the current official ICC text before relying on it for a live transaction, guarantee, or letter of credit.
